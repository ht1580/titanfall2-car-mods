Titanfall Mod Workbench invokes or bundles independent third-party tools.

RSX / reSource Xtractor: https://github.com/r-ex/rsx (AGPL-3.0)
LegionPlus: https://github.com/r-ex/LegionPlus
RePak: https://github.com/r-ex/RePak
Crowbar: https://steamcommunity.com/groups/CrowbarTool
Microsoft DirectXTex texconv: https://github.com/microsoft/DirectXTex
Harmony VPK Tool: distributed as an independent executable by its author.
MDLShit: distributed as an independent Titanfall model conversion utility.
VTFEdit Reloaded / VTFCmd and VTFLib: used for VTF texture generation;
copyright and license remain with their respective authors.
Blender Source Tools + Batch SMD 3.4.4: Copyright (c) 2014 Tom Edwards and
Batch SMD contributors; GNU GPL version 2 or any later version. Corresponding
Python source is included in vendor/blender/io_scene_valvesource.

Blender, Source Filmmaker, Titanfall 2 and Apex Legends are not embedded. The
Workbench only invokes installations selected by the user.
